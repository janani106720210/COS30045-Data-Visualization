# Exercise 3 – Data Story: TV Energy Consumption

## Overview

The TV Energy Consumption dataset is used in this assignment to create a data story. The webpage from Exercise 0.2 is expanded to include supporting text and visualizations of the findings.

The objective is to assist people comprehend TV screen sizes and energy use by simply communicating the data.
---

## Data Story

### Audience

Consumers who intend to buy a television are the target audience.

They want to comprehend:

- Which TV screen sizes are most frequently included in the dataset?
- Things to think about while selecting a TV data set?
- How screen size is related to energy consumption?


### Story Overview

Two questions are used in this data story to investigate TV screen size and energy consumption:

1. What is the frequency of each TV size?
2. What effect does screen size have on energy usage?

The distribution of TV screen sizes is displayed using a histogram, and the correlation between screen size and labeled energy usage is displayed using a scatter plot.

To aid consumers in comprehending the results, the visualizations are accompanied by explanatory text.

---

## About the Data

### Data Source

The course materials included the TV Energy Consumption dataset. It includes details regarding television models, such as screen dimensions and energy usage.

### Data Processing

KNIME was used to explore and process the data. Appropriate variables were chosen to plot a histogram of the distribution of screen sizes and a scatter plot of screen size against labelled energy consumption.

### Privacy

The data set refers to television products, and does not include any personal or sensitive data about individuals.

### Accuracy and Limitations

This data might not be available for all TVs on the market. The results are dependent on the correctness and completeness of the data supplied. There may also be differences in energy consumption between models of TVs that have similar screen sizes.

### Ethics

The drawings should be an accurate representation of the data and not lead to incorrect conclusions. Findings relate to the data available, and may not be relied upon for making unsupported statements about TV brands, models or the overall television market.
